Expertise

Since starting my Master's thesis in 2014, I have been exploring *deep learning* approaches to solve problems related to *3D character animation*. These applications include *motion classification, posing, motion completion, motion generation* and *text-to-motion*.

I have built and trained *convolutional, recurrent, attention* and *prototypical neural networks* in settings such as *supervised* and *semi-supervised learning, auto-regressive prediction, auto-encoding* and *variational auto-encoding, generative adversarial models*, and *diffusion/flow matching*. My current focus is on building state-of-the-art real-time motion generation models, in highly constrained settings for in-game usage.
